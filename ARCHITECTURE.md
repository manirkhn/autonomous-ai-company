# Autonomous AI Company: Foundation Architecture Blueprint

## Mission
"Discover profitable legitimate opportunities, build and sell digital products or services, automate the majority of operations, measure real business results, and continuously improve using AI."

## Core Principles & Hard Guardrails
1. **Human Ownership & Financial Firewall**:
   - The human owner retains exclusive control over all real banking, funds, and financial credentials.
   - Hardcoded zero-trust rule: AI cannot initiate transfers, withdrawals, loans, credential updates, or binding commitments without explicit human authorization.
   - Default spending limit: $0.00 approval threshold (every expenditure requires an approved Human Approval Request).
   - Emergency Kill Switch: Instantly halts all AI tasks, external calls, and automations.
2. **Accountability & Evidence-Based Completion**:
   - No task is marked `COMPLETED` without tangible evidence (links, outputs, test receipts, diffs).
   - Structured handoffs replace uncontrolled conversations.
   - Full audit logging: every critical action, tool call, approval, and decision is cryptographically logged.
3. **Legitimate, Lawful & Safe Operations**:
   - Zero tolerance for spam, deception, platform abuse, impersonation, or copyright infringement.
   - Built-in Compliance & Risk Agent that screens opportunities and workflows.
4. **Owner Time Minimization**:
   - Every human intervention is logged with root cause analysis (why owner was needed, what tool/skill was missing, how to automate).

## Pipeline Lifecycle
```
RESEARCH 
  → OPPORTUNITY DISCOVERY 
  → PRODUCT/SERVICE DESIGN 
  → PRODUCT CREATION 
  → QUALITY CONTROL (QA & Compliance) 
  → MARKETING 
  → SALES 
  → CUSTOMER ONBOARDING 
  → DELIVERY 
  → CUSTOMER SUPPORT 
  → FINANCE ANALYSIS 
  → BUSINESS OPTIMIZATION 
  → AUTOMATION 
  → NEW SKILL ACQUISITION 
  → EXPANSION
```
